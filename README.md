### Paisa Trail

A private Android expense tracker that turns the SMS your banks already send into an **encrypted, on-device ledger**. Budgets with 80% and 100% alerts, splits with friends and Splitwise sync, foreign spends at ECB reference rates, a review queue for anything the parser doubts, and a home-screen widget. No bank login, no account aggregator, no analytics. An optional AI reader sees only masked text.

<sub>ANDROID · KOTLIN · JETPACK COMPOSE · SQLCIPHER · SELF-HOSTED SPLITWISE BRIDGE (TYPESCRIPT · DOCKER)</sub> &nbsp; <sub>Source is private for now.</sub>

## How I work across the stack

- **AI with verifiable results.** Small models that turn messy text into structured data, and development workflows with explicit review gates.
